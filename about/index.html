About Us

"Watermelon Time" is an independent hobby project run by a resident of Albuquerque, New Mexico - not a company or commercial service.

Using live weather data the application determines if the time is right to check our web camera and uses computer vision to determine if it's the ideal time for you to snap a picture or take in the natural beauty of the Sandias. When the conditions are right we send you an sms message.

There is no sign up costs or ads.
To stop receiving messages reply stop to any message.
