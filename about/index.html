About Us

"Watermelon Time" is an independent hobby project run by a resident of Albuquerque, New Mexico - not a company or commercial service.

Using live weather data the project determines if the time is right to check a camera and uses computer vision to determine if it's the ideal time for you to snap a picture or take in the natural beauty of the Sandias.

There is no sign up costs or ads.
