Is It Watermelon Time?

The word Sandia means watermelon in Spanish. The Spanish first entering the Rio Grande Valley in what is now Albuquerque New Mexico may have given that name to the mountains because at sunset they glow an incredibly bright pink. My family likes to call this phenomenon:
🍉 "Watermelon Time" 🌄

The exact time changes throughout the year, and is highly dependent on the weather/clouds. If you want to know if the Sandia's are glowing, signup to get a SMS notification straight to your pocket.

No text if it's cloudy or rainy. Just one message at the exact time to go outside and look East.
